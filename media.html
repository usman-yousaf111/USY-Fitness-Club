Weekly Workout Plan

Here is our weekly workout schedule.

Day Muscle Groups
MondayChest, Triceps
TuesdayBack, Biceps
WednesdayShoulders, Abs
ThursdayChest, Triceps
FridayBack, Biceps
SaturdayShoulders, Legs
SundayRest Day

Monday - Chest and Triceps

  • Bench Press
  • Push-Ups
  • Dumbbell Press
  • Tricep Pushdowns
  • Tricep Extensions

Start with a proper warm-up.

Tuesday - Back and Biceps

  • Lat Pulldowns
  • Seated Rows
  • Dumbbell Rows
  • Bicep Curls
  • Hammer Curls

Keep every movement controlled.

Wednesday - Shoulders and Abs

  • Shoulder Press
  • Lateral Raises
  • Front Raises
  • Planks
  • Crunches

Use weights you can control safely.

Thursday - Chest and Triceps

  • Incline Press
  • Push-Ups
  • Chest Fly
  • Tricep Extensions
  • Close-Grip Press

Focus on correct form.

Friday - Back and Biceps

  • Lat Pulldowns
  • Seated Rows
  • Back Extensions
  • Barbell Curls
  • Hammer Curls

Do not rush your repetitions.

Saturday - Shoulders and Legs

  • Shoulder Press
  • Lateral Raises
  • Squats
  • Leg Press
  • Leg Curls

Warm up before lifting weights.

Sunday - Rest Day

  • Take time to recover.
  • Drink enough water.
  • Eat balanced meals.
  • Get enough sleep.
  • Prepare for the next week.

Workout Reminders

  • Warm up before exercise.
  • Stay hydrated during training.
  • Use equipment carefully.
  • Rest between sets.
  • Ask for help if needed.
  • Never ignore pain or dizziness.
